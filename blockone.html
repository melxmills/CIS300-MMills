Block I: The Beginning of My Journey

August 18 – September 29, 2025

Block I marked the beginning of my study abroad experience in London. During these first six weeks, I started getting comfortable with living in a new country, exploring London, meeting new friends, and traveling throughout Europe. This block included some of my first major experiences abroad, including my first trip with friends and my first solo travel experience.

Living in London

London was my home base throughout Block I, and I spent a lot of time exploring the city and trying new experiences. Some of my favorite activities included taking pottery classes, seeing The Phantom of the Opera on the West End, visiting Kew Gardens, and getting the opportunity to go inside Buckingham Palace.

I also took day trips outside of London to Brighton and Oxford. These trips gave me the opportunity to see more of England while still being able to return to London at the end of the day.

Paris, France

From September 4–9, I traveled to Paris for my first trip outside of London with five of the friends I met while studying abroad. We spent five days exploring the city and visiting many of its famous landmarks.

I booked several excursions during the trip, including the Palace of Versailles, the Louvre Museum, and a sunset cruise along the Seine River. We also explored Notre-Dame, Galeries Lafayette, and the jazz club that inspired La La Land.

My First Solo Adventure: Normandy

While my friends returned to London, I stayed in France for an extra day and completed my first solo travel experience. I joined a group excursion from Paris to Normandy for a D-Day tour.

We traveled by bus and visited Utah Beach and Omaha Beach. We also visited the Normandy American Cemetery and an abandoned underground bunker. This was one of the most memorable experiences of Block I because it was both an important historical experience and the first time I traveled on my own while abroad.

Copenhagen, Denmark

My final international trip of Block I was Copenhagen from September 19–21. During my time there, I visited Tivoli Gardens, explored Nyhavn, tried Danish hot dogs, and visited the royal palace.

Copenhagen was a great way to finish the travel portion of my first block and gave me another opportunity to experience a completely different European city and culture.
