Block II: Exploring Even Further

September 30 - November 8, 2025

Block II was filled with even more travel and new experiences. I continued exploring London and other parts of England while also traveling to Serbia, Scotland, and Norway. During these weeks, I saw more West End productions, visited historic landmarks, completed my first true solo trip, traveled through the Scottish Highlands, and even crossed into the Arctic Circle.

London & England

I continued exploring London throughout Block II and experienced even more of what the city had to offer. On October 1, I saw Harry Potter and the Cursed Child. The London production was especially memorable because I got to experience the original five-hour production.

On October 3, I visited the Sky Garden and got to see London from above. A few days later, on October 8, I returned to the West End to see Hadestown.

From October 10–11, I traveled to Bath for an overnight trip with my study abroad program. On October 14, I had one of my most unique experiences in London when I visited Parliament. I was able to meet members of Parliament and receive a private tour of the government building.

On October 18, I also visited Windsor Castle, adding another famous British landmark to my experiences in England.

Belgrade, Serbia

From October 15–17, I traveled to Belgrade, Serbia. This was my first true solo trip, which made it a very different experience from my previous travels. Traveling completely on my own gave me the opportunity to become more independent and confident navigating a new country.

While in Belgrade, I explored the Belgrade Fortress and visited the Church of Saint Sava. Serbia was a unique addition to my study abroad travels and gave me the chance to experience another culture and city completely on my own.

Edinburgh & the Scottish Highlands

From October 22–26, I traveled to Edinburgh, Scotland. Scotland quickly became one of my favorite places that I visited while abroad.

From October 23–24, I went on an Outlander tour through the Scottish Highlands. The trip included Glencoe, the Culloden Battlefield, and other locations throughout the Highlands. I stayed overnight in Inverness before continuing the tour.

The scenery throughout the Highlands made this one of the most memorable trips of my entire study abroad experience. Between Edinburgh, Inverness, Glencoe, and the Highlands, Scotland became one of my favorite destinations from my time in Europe.

Tromsø, Norway

From November 4–6, I traveled north to Tromsø, Norway. This trip was completely different from the other places I had visited because it took me above the Arctic Circle.

One of the highlights of the trip was going on a whale-watching expedition in the Arctic Ocean. I also visited the world's northernmost McDonald's and received a certificate recognizing that I had traveled above the Arctic Circle.

Visiting Tromsø was an unforgettable way to finish the major travels of Block II and gave me an experience unlike anywhere else I visited during my semester abroad.
